Terms

  • Deposit: 50% non-refundable up front to book your slot. Balance due on final approval, before full-resolution delivery.
  • Timeline: most pieces take 3–4 weeks depending on complexity.
  • Revisions: 2 rounds at the sketch stage are included. Changes requested after painting begins may add cost.
  • Rights: full personal use of the final piece is included. Commercial licenses (book covers, games, merchandise) are available at twice the list price.
  • Payment: through Ko-fi, no fees, secure checkout.
  • Credit: please credit @tallangrobergart when sharing.
  • No AI: all work is hand-crafted. Your commission will never touch generative AI.
