Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom. Displacement is live and non destructive until you bake it: baking makes the current sculpture your new base, and undo steps back. Uploads keep their own mesh density, so dense models take the fractal best. STL export is ready for a 3D printer. The pattern menu has a whole nature shelf: coral colonies that cluster in patches, honeycomb walls, branching veins, sedimentary terraces, overlapping scales, crystal clusters, and wind blown dunes. Bake one, then grow another on top. Or skip sculpting a shape by hand entirely: the Generate menu grows whole forms from natural recipes, ant nests with chambers and tunnels, termite mounds, coral, trees, lightning, colony clusters. Complexity fattens the recipe, New seed rerolls the dice, and every grown form still takes displacement, baking, and export like any other base. The ant nest digs three ways: random walk, A* goal seeking through noisy rock, or a spanning tree web. Venation grows by space colonization, branches chasing scattered points of attraction, and the dendrite grows by diffusion limited aggregation, wanderers sticking where they touch. Generated detail trades patience for fineness. Forking decides how soon branches split: low grows tall and sparse, high forks almost at the trunk and crowds the canopy. The Painted picture pattern wraps one of your images around the form by longitude and latitude and raises the bright country: open any picture, or start with the Serenitas already loaded. Tint from picture wears the image as skin where the form has UVs.
