FRACTAL EDGES

warp the edges of an image with fractal noise, live

Sample loaded: Serenitas

Drag and drop an image anywhere on the page. Distortion concentrates where the image has edges, broken up by layered fractal noise. Relief depth shades the picture like a lit sculpture, and fractal depth decides how much of that relief comes from the fractal instead of the picture itself. Move your pointer across the image to tilt it. Where edges meet at corners, the tool can grow little Romanesco style fractal buds, cones inside cones with spiral ribs, shaded by the same relief light. The feedback button runs the picture through again so the effect keeps growing. The pattern menu swaps the fractal equation behind the warp. Seam carving reshapes the width: set a percentage, hit apply, and it removes or duplicates the least noticeable winding path through the picture. Undo steps back through your input changes.
