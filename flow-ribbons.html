THE CURRENT (an invisible flow field)
THE STRANDS (ribbons riding the current)

Under every ribbon here is an invisible current, a smooth mathematical wind that swirls the whole volume. Release a strand anywhere in it and the current carries the strand downstream, bending, braiding, and folding it around its neighbors. Freeze the moment and you have wind carved fins, drifting kelp, or energy hair, all as one solid mesh. Source shape chooses where the strands are born: a fountain disc throws them upward like a plume, an orb cloud wraps them around a sphere, a ring gate pours them through a halo, and a falling veil drops them in a sheet. Flow scale tightens or loosens the swirls, field phase rotates the whole weather system into a new arrangement, and upward drift biases the current toward the sky or the deep. New seed reshuffles the birthplaces inside the same weather. Export GLB takes the frozen drift into Nomad Sculpt with vertex colors that fade along each strand; STL covers printers. Solid thickness now gives every strand a real body with capped ends, ready to sculpt and far friendlier to print; set it to zero and the strands go back to pure sheets. Curl currents replace the old sine weather with layered, braiding flow, and Painted picture coloring lets you load one of your own paintings so each strand is born wearing its colors.

A word on exporting: ribbons are thin sheets, so they sculpt and render beautifully and print best with a little width on them. If a strand ever looks too fragile, Ribbon width is the fix, not strand count.
